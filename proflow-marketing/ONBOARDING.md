# First-client onboarding playbook

Use this checklist the first time a real paying client comes onto the
platform. The whole thing should take about 30 minutes if you have their
brand assets ready.

## Inside the app

1. **Clients &rarr; + Add Client.** Fill in:
   - Client name, primary contact name, billing email.
   - Industry (closest match).
   - Active platforms and the monthly content quota per platform
     (default 12, change as needed).
   - Brand colors (primary + accent) &mdash; lift from their site.
   - Assign the strategists who will work on the account.
   - Save. Resend fires an invitation email to the primary contact.
2. **Clients &rarr; [the client] &rarr; Brand.** Paste in:
   - About the brand (1-2 paragraphs).
   - Target audience (1 paragraph, demographics + psychographics).
   - Brand voice and tone with "Words we use" and "Words we avoid"
     lists.
   - Visual identity (color swatches, logo filenames, font names).
   - Content pillars (3-5).
   - Dos and Don'ts.
   - Hashtag strategy (Branded / Niche / Broad).
   - Competitors and key links.
3. **Asset Library &rarr; Upload Assets.** Drop in:
   - Logos (Logos &amp; Branding folder).
   - Lifestyle and product photography.
   - Any approved video clips.
   - Add tags so search works later.
4. **Content Calendar &rarr; + New Post.** Draft 1-2 posts that exercise
   your most common formats (reel, carousel, etc). Save as Draft and
   send for approval so the client experiences the approval flow on
   day one.
5. **Settings &rarr; Payment Methods.** Make sure your Wise / Bank /
   Payoneer details are filled in. The client&apos;s first invoice pulls
   from here.
6. **Invoices &rarr; Recurring Invoices &rarr; + New Recurring Schedule.**
   Monthly on day 1 (or whichever day fits the client&apos;s billing
   cycle). Set Auto-send on if you want it to fire without review.

## Email the client

A short welcome email with:

- The portal URL (`https://app.proflow.example`).
- A line that they will receive a separate "Set your password" email
  from Supabase / Resend &mdash; that link expires in 1 hour.
- What to expect in week one (approvals, first report timing, billing
  cadence).
- Your direct contact info.

Attach the "Getting Started" 2-pager once it exists.

## During week one

- Watch the dashboard&apos;s **Needs your attention** column for stuck
  approvals.
- Hit **/analytics** to confirm baseline follower counts seeded
  correctly. If not, use Update metrics or Import from CSV.
- At the end of the month, the Reports page auto-generates the first
  monthly summary. Review it before the client opens it &mdash;
  edit "What is coming next month" while you're in there.

## Things to avoid

- Don&apos;t skip the Brand Guidelines page. The AI Caption Assistant
  uses it; quality drops without it.
- Don&apos;t hand-build invoices for a client who has a recurring
  schedule &mdash; you&apos;ll end up with duplicates.
- Don&apos;t reuse client invitation links across multiple clients
  &mdash; each link is single-use.
